1.
Search trigger: "gooseneck kettle temperature control pour over" — highly competitive keyword set

2.

3.

4.
Trust phase: Reviews validate real-world temperature accuracy, speed, reliability

5.
Conversion risk: Buyer hesitates at brand recognition gap vs. Cosori/Bonavita; reliability concerns surface in reviews

- Listing Gaps & Optimization Opportunities

A. Title Issues
Current title:
"Electric Gooseneck Kettle Temperature Control, Pour Over Electric Kettle for Coffee and Tea, 100% Stainless Steel Inner, 1200W Rapid Heating, 0.8L, Built-in Stopwatch, Matte Black"
Problems:

•
Brand name "Aiheal" is absent from the title — hurts brand recall and repeat searches

•
"Temperature Control" appears before the brand — wasted prime real estate

•
No mention of ±1°F or 100–212°F range in title, which are strong conversion signals

•
"Built-in Stopwatch" is buried at end

Suggested title direction:
"Aiheal Gooseneck Electric Kettle, ±1°F Temperature Control 100°F–212°F, Pour Over Coffee & Tea, 1200W, 0.8L, Built-in Brew Timer, Matte Black"
